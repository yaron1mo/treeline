# Mistral squads-2 — helicopter and drone waves, cost band 6000–12000, measured again

Task: issue #57, follow-up of #23 / PR #56 (`docs/balance/mistral/squads/report.md`). Date: 2026-11-28. Decision by Yaroslav (2026-11-28): first better tests, **no game value changed** (`stop.csv`, `units.csv`, `armour.csv`, `waves.csv`, `battle_rules.csv`, Sim code). The deliverable is the measurement and a comparison with PR #56.

## Method

Same method as PR #56:

- Tool: `WaveCost.cs` squad mode (`SQUADS_FILE`), run by the `Balance run` workflow (mono on `ubuntu-latest`, same sources). For each squad the scripted player can buy only the defender types of that squad. `cost` is `NaN` when the squad cannot hold the wave with `max_budget` 12000. Each wave is fought alone on an empty field; the cost is the smallest budget (step 25) that holds the wave on at least 8 of 10 seeds.
- Wave set: `tools/balance/squad_waves.csv` — 85 waves. Waves 1–40 are the unchanged PR #56 set (mixed probe waves, measured cost 500–6000 on Forest belts). Waves 41–85 are new (this task):
  - waves 41–45: helicopter waves with 2 helicopters each (43 and 44 also carry old_tank) — **5 waves with 2+ helicopters**, measured cost of `all` on Forest belts 6475–10425 (band 6000–12000);
  - waves 46–48: drone-attacker waves (recon_uav spotting + 4–8 fpv + infantry) in the 500–6000 band; waves 49–50: drone waves with one `one_way_drone` each plus one helicopter — **5 drone-attacker waves** (recon_uav + fpv; waves 49–50 also one_way_drone);
  - waves 51–85: mixed waves generated by `make_waves.py` (seeds 11 and 23) and tuned locally (WaveCost, no squads, Forest belts) until the measured cost landed in 6500–11500.
  - Cost bands on Forest belts (measured cost of the squad `all` in the squad runs): **500–6000: 45 waves** (1–40 + 46–50), **6000–12000: 40 waves** (41–45 + 51–85), none "not held" on Forest belts. The 500–6000 and 6000–12000 bands have about the same number of waves (45 vs 40).
  - `one_way_drone`: the Sim has it (`fly_to_rear` in `Battle.cs`, and `units.csv` gives it `targetable_by = air_defence` — only air_defence can shoot it), so waves 49–50 include it.
- Squads: `make_squads.py` writes every squad of N types from the 9 defence types plus `all`. N = 3, 4, 5 (one Balance run per size). Squad name = short type names joined by `+`.
- Runs (`Balance run` on branch `mistral/squads-2`, `run.json`: waves_file `squad_waves.csv`, squads_file `squads.csv`, max_budget 12000, playtest_seeds 0, fields = the 3 fields of PR #56: Balka and railway, Forest belts, River crossing):

- Analysis: `analyze.py` (this folder) reads the merged `wave-cost.csv` of the runs and writes per size, field and band: held share, cost premium (median of squad cost / cost of `all` on the waves the squad holds), rank; per defender type: must-have, best-10 share, unused. Dominant = held share ≥ 0.90 and premium ≤ 1.2 (the dominant lists below exclude the squad `all`; PR #56 counted it, so the comparison gives both numbers). Bands are set by the cost of `all` on Forest belts per wave.
- The 2 village fields (Crossroads, Village street) are not fields of PR #56 and are out of scope of this task.

What changed and what did not:

1. The low band still has many dominant squads of 4 on Forest belts and River crossing (26 and 19 incl. `all`, vs 23 and 27 in PR #56) — every one of them contains anti_tank. The new drone waves did not remove the dominance; they added waves where air_defence is needed (49–50).
2. The new high band 6000–12000 is where the squad choice starts to matter: on River crossing no 4-squad is dominant (best held share 0.42) and on Balka and railway `all` holds only 0.07 at 12000. On Forest belts the high band is still held by `all` and 15 other squads — anti_tank is must-have for 65% of those waves.
3. air_defence got a measured role from the new waves: best-10 share 0.98 in the low band on Forest belts (PR #56: in the best 10 of 0% of helicopter waves), must-have 0.04 (the one_way_drone waves). But on the 2-helicopter waves 41–45 it is in the best 10 for 0 of 10 on every field — the helicopter is killed cheaper by other guns (stop.csv: machine_gun 0.25, sniper 0.22, rifle_squad 0.10 vs air_defence 0.80 at build cost 500 vs 100–200).
4. machine_gun is still the most unused type (unused 0.53 on River crossing low band, PR #56: 0.475).

## Proposals 1–3 of PR #56 — do the new data support them?

The proposals stay proposals only; no value was changed.

1. **Give armour a second counter (fpv_operator vs modern_tank 1.00→2.00, vs old_tank 2.43→3.00): supported.** In the 6000–12000 band, anti_tank is must-have for 65% of the waves on Forest belts and 35% on River crossing, and every dominant squad of 4 contains it. fpv_operator is in the best 10 of 100% of the high-band waves on Forest belts but never makes a squad dominant without anti_tank — exactly the "anti_tank in every dominant squad" problem the proposal addresses.
2. **Make machine_gun the clear anti-elite pick (machine_gun vs special_forces 2.43→4.00): supported.** machine_gun is still the most unused type (unused share 0.20–0.53 per field and band; worst River crossing low band 0.53, PR #56 0.475). The new helicopter and drone waves did not give it a role.
3. **Make air_defence worth its 500 cost on helicopter waves (air_defence vs helicopter 0.80→2.00): the wave-adding alternative is now tested and did not help; the value change is supported.** PR #56 rested on a single 1-helicopter wave. This task added 5 waves with 2+ helicopters (41–45): air_defence is in the best 10 for 0 of them on all 3 fields. Its only must-have role is vs one_way_drone (waves 49–50, 10/10 in the best 10), which no other type can target. So more helicopter waves do not fix it; the stop.csv change (or a cheaper air_defence) is the lever the data points to.

## Strange results
